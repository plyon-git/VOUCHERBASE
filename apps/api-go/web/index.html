Tenant-paid utilities

Select only applicable tenant responsibilities. Mutually exclusive fuel/septic choices cannot be combined. Tenant appliance allowances are separate.

Unknown voucher entitlement produces a unit-size planning reference only. Household assistance and approval are not calculated.
03 / FINANCIAL ASSUMPTIONS

All financial defaults are illustrative assumptions. NOI excludes financing and capital reserves; cash flow includes them. Utilities paid by the owner are an operating cost, not the tenant allowance.

Includes base, rent −5%, vacancy 10% and renovation +20% scenarios.
